The Origin

Why Sovereign City Exists.

A letter from Millionz on freedom, optionality, digital sovereignty, and building a life by design — not by default.

Read the Letter
Freedom rarely disappears overnight.It fades gradually.

Freedom rarely disappears overnight.

More often, it fades gradually—one policy, one regulation, one digital system at a time.

Over the past several years, I've watched that process accelerate across much of the Western world.

The expansion of digital surveillance, online censorship, financial oversight, digital identity initiatives, and the steady erosion of personal privacy have raised an increasingly important question:

What will freedom look like in the decades ahead?
For me, it became personal

The Decision

For many people, these developments remain abstract policy discussions.

For me, they became personal.

Alongside my wife and family, I made the decision to leave the United Kingdom and relocate to Mexico—not because Mexico is perfect, and not because I was running away from anything—but because I wanted greater optionality, greater freedom, and the opportunity to build a life more aligned with my own values.

That decision changed the direction of my life.

It set me on a journey into the worlds of privacy, digital sovereignty, self-custody, and decentralized technology.

The Journey

Finding Clarity

Over the years, I've attended conferences across the privacy and cryptocurrency space, immersed myself in emerging technologies, tested countless tools, and spent hundreds of hours researching everything from GrapheneOS and encrypted communications to Monero, self-custody, digital identity systems, and the future of financial sovereignty.

Along the way, I realised something.

The real challenge wasn't finding information.

It was finding clarity.

The answers existed—but they were scattered across podcasts, conference talks, research papers, forums, X threads, YouTube videos, and technical documentation.

Finding genuinely useful information often meant spending dozens of hours piecing together fragments from dozens of different places.

Not another news website.

Sovereign City exists to help people build lives with greater freedom, resilience, and optionality in an increasingly digital world.
The Goal

Clarity

Here you'll find practical ideas, thoughtful analysis, trusted resources, and proven tools designed to help you make informed decisions in a rapidly changing world.

We'll explore privacy, financial sovereignty, decentralised technologies, AI, international diversification, health, resilience, and the habits that help people remain adaptable as the future unfolds.

But above all else, we'll focus on practical action.

The goal is not fear.
The goal is not outrage.
And the goal is certainly not political tribalism.

The goal is clarity.

The Promise

Complete Honesty

I don't claim to have all the answers.

What I can promise is this:

I'll continue researching, testing, questioning, and sharing what I learn with complete honesty.

If I discover something genuinely useful, I'll tell you.

If something doesn't live up to the hype, I'll tell you that too.

Sovereign City isn't about escaping the world.

It's about engaging with it thoughtfully, building options, and making conscious decisions rather than simply accepting defaults.

If that resonates with you, then you're exactly where you belong.
